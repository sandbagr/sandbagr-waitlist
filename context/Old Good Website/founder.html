I picked up a golf club at five years old. By high school, my friends and I were running skins games every weekend. By college, every Saturday round ended the same way: arguments on 18, Venmo IOUs that disappeared, and mental math that three different guys remembered three different ways.

THE SANDBAGR isn't a startup idea I researched. It's years of a problem I lived with every single round. Betting with my boys and watching the same chaos play out on 18 every time.

The Origin
Every golfer knows the problem.
Nobody was solving it.
01

The Problem Was Personal

Every round with my foursome involved some version of the same chaos: who's up, what are the odds, did Jake actually hit that fairway, and who owes who at the end. We'd spend more time arguing the bet than playing the hole. I figured every golfer dealt with this. Turns out, they do.

02

The Pivot

I originally pitched a different golf app, CaddyAI, at UVA's Entrepreneurship Cup. Won the whole thing. But during discovery, I analyzed 18+ golf analytics competitors and found something wild: not a single app served the social betting side of golf. Zero. I pivoted overnight.

03

The Build

Five months of solo-founder execution. 200+ pages of product documentation. A complete rebrand. A 25-person focus group that came back with 100% download intent. IP counsel. Developer recruited. I didn't raise a dollar. I gained access to $140K+ in in-kind prizes and started building.
