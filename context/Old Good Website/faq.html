Polymarket-style YES/NO contracts on round outcomes, opened the night before tee time. "Lowest score today?" "Anyone goes hole-in-one?" "Mike vs. Jake H2H?" Group prices the line by trading in. You buy YES at 62¢, the line moves, you trade out or hold to settlement. Round resolves contracts from the scorecard. League positions settle on the 1st of every month via debt-minimized Venmo set — one tap per pair, not chasing 24 people.

A logistic regression model weighs real-time inputs: GHIN handicap, distance, lie, club yardages, hole context, personal history, and momentum — then converts that probability to a decimal multiplier with a thin vig built in. On Hole 1 we use public data from 600M+ real shots. By Round 5, your personal stats take over. By Round 20, the engine knows your game better than you do.

In-round bets settle the same day on the 19th Hole screen between your foursome. Pre-round market positions are different — they often span the whole league (24+ people across multiple foursomes). Chasing 24 Venmo settlements is bad UX. So markets auto-settle on the 1st of every month: the app debt-minimizes the entire league's positions, sends each pair one Venmo deep-link, and you clear it in seconds. One ritual day a month, instead of one chase per round.
