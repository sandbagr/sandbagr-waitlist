How it works

Three taps from trash talk to a paid tab.

SANDBAGR isn't a sportsbook. It's the scorekeeper for the bets you and your buddies were already making. The app holds the memory, not the money.

01

Make the bet

Agree the buy-in, pick a format, and fire bets mid-round. Skins, presses, closest-to-the-pin, "$5 says you chunk it." If you can talk it, you can track it.

02

Play the round

Bets resolve live on a shared ledger as the round happens. Everyone sees who's up, who's down, and who's quietly sandbagging the back nine.

03

Settle the tab

One number at the 19th hole. Tap to settle, send it straight to Venmo. No napkin math, no "I'll get you next time" that never comes.
