About us

Built by a golfer
tired of the chaos on 18.

SANDBAGR started as a personal grievance. It's on the App Store now.

Strava made running social. Polymarket made markets accessible. SANDBAGR is what happens when both live in the same app.

Colin Critchley, Founder

The founder

Colin Critchley

Wrote every word. Designed every screen. Lost the bets that started it.

I picked up a club at five. By high school I was running skins games every weekend. By college, every Saturday round ended the same way: arguments on 18, Venmo IOUs that never landed, mental math three guys remembered three different ways.

SANDBAGR isn't a startup idea I researched. It's a problem I lived through. Every. Single. Round.

The road

Idea to the App Store.

No outside capital. A few milestones that compounded.

Nov 2025
Won UVA's E-Cup.
Beat 40+ teams in the B2C category. Took home $40K+ in prizes, startup credits, and full Foundry access.
Dec 2025
Pivoted overnight.
Looked at 18+ golf-tech competitors. Analytics was crowded. Social betting had zero players. Killed the old idea and rebuilt the deck in 36 hours.
Mar 2026
Brand locked.
The SANDBAGR name. The mascot. 200+ pages of product docs before a line of code.
Apr 2026
Dev team assembled.
Developers on milestone-based comp. IP locked. The prototype became the spec.
Sep 2026
Live on the App Store.
Version 1.0 passed App Review and shipped. Download it.
